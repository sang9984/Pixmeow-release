# Roamie

> A pixel cat that roams freely on your macOS desktop.

Roamie is a desktop pet for macOS. A pixel-art cat lives on your screen — it walks, sleeps, eats, plays, and reacts to you. Inspired by the Shimeji tradition.

## Features

- 🐾 **Free roaming** — your cat walks across the screen on its own schedule
- 🍽️ **Vital stats** — fullness, fatigue, and stress decay over time; feed, sleep, and play to recover
- 😾 **Reactive behavior** — drag a stressed cat and it hisses; let it run too tired and it forces a nap
- 🎨 **Multiple cat variants** — pick gray, white, orange, brown, or bicolor
- 🌍 **13 languages** — Korean, English, Japanese, Simplified/Traditional Chinese, Thai, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Spanish, French, German, Russian, Brazilian Portuguese
- 🪟 **Menu bar app** — runs quietly, never gets in the way
- 🖼️ **Always-on-top** or hide-on-fullscreen — fits any workflow

## Requirements

- macOS 13 (Ventura) or later
- Apple Silicon or Intel

## Download

Get the latest version from the [Releases page](https://github.com/sang9984/Roamie-release/releases).

1. Download `Roamie-x.y.z.dmg`
2. Open the DMG and drag Roamie into your Applications folder
3. Launch Roamie — a cat will appear on your screen
